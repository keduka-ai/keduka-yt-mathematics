## About Keduka

This repository is part of **[Keduka AI School (KAIS)](https://keduka.com)**, an AI-powered learning platform by **Keduka Cognitive Services (KCS)**. KAIS pairs every learner with adaptive AI teachers across data science, machine learning, NLP, computer vision, federated learning, physics, chemistry, biology, and mathematics.

> Learn AI the **fun** way — personalized tutoring that adapts to your pace.

The **Keduka YT — Mathematics** project is the open curriculum behind the mathematics track on our YouTube channel: a structured, end-to-end roadmap from elementary arithmetic to advanced university-level mathematics and its direct applications in AI, Deep Learning, and Computational Physics.

## Curriculum

The curriculum is organized into six parts, progressing through levels of complexity while highlighting the relevance of each topic to Machine Learning.

```text
├── Part_I_Foundations/
│   ├── Chapter_01_Arithmetic/
│   └── ...
├── Part_II_...
├── Part_III_...
├── Part_IV_...
├── Part_V_...
└── Part_VI_...
```

Each chapter ships alongside a YouTube lesson on the Keduka AI School channel and links back to interactive lessons inside the KAIS platform.

## How to use this repo

- **Watch** the matching lesson on the Keduka YouTube channel.
- **Read** the chapter notes in this repository for the written reference.
- **Practice** with the exercises in each chapter folder.
- **Go deeper** — open the same topic inside [keduka.com](https://keduka.com) for adaptive practice with your AI tutor.
